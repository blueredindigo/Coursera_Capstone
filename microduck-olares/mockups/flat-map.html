What happened

    What's mocked: the floor plan, the ducks' walking pace (sped up so you can watch), and what they see (a simple line-of-sight cone). What's real to the plan: 25 cm fog cells, Reachy's view clearing the lounge, the anchor tags that reset a duck's position, trips cut short by battery or distance since the last tag, the two ducks splitting rooms, closed doors pausing a room, and old information staying until someone looks again.
